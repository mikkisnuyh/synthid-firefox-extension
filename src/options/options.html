SynthID Check

Checks images, video and audio on any website for a SynthID watermark, the invisible mark that Google's AI tools and partners such as OpenAI add to what they generate. The file is handed to Google's official detector at synthid.com, where you sign in and see the result.

How to use

  • Right-click an image, video or audio element and choose Check with SynthID.
  • If a site covers its media with an overlay, right-click the spot and choose Find media under the cursor and check with SynthID.
  • Drag an image and drop it on the box that appears in the bottom-right corner.
  • Or click the toolbar button, choose Pick media on this page, then click the media. Alt+Shift+S starts this directly.

Only SynthID watermarks can be detected. If none is found, that doesn't prove the media was made by a person. Text can't be checked.

General

While you drag an image, a box appears in the bottom-right corner. Drop the image on it to check it. Changing this applies to pages you open or reload afterwards.

Website access

To check a file, SynthID Check downloads the original from the site that hosts it, which is often a different site than the page you're on (for example an image CDN). That's why it needs access to all websites. Files are only downloaded when you choose to check them.
